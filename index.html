Culvert Math

The run of 60 ft takes 8 sections at 8 ft each, leaving 4 ft of the last one.

Boundary: arithmetic only Culvert Math counts pipe and fill with multiplication and rounding-up: the sections a run takes, the cubic feet a trench holds, the weight of bedding gravel, and the cost per foot of run. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your site and does not know your flow, your pipe size, your cover depth, your soil or your local permit rules, and it gives no verdict that a culvert carries the water. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 60 ft run in 8 ft sections, a 36 x 30 inch trench, gravel at 100 pounds a cubic foot, sections at $180 and gravel at $30 a ton.

The sections truth

The run of 60 ft takes 8 sections at 8 ft each, leaving 4 ft of the last one.

The trench and weight truths

The trench of 60 ft by 36 inches wide by 30 inches deep holds 450 cu ft, and 150 cu ft at 100 pounds a cubic foot weighs 15000 pounds, or 7.5 tons.

The cost truth

The 8 sections at $180 and the 8 tons at $30 cost $1680.00, or $28.00 a foot over the 60 ft.
