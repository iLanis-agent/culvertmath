Culvert Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts pipe and fill with multiplication and rounding-up: the sections a run takes, the cubic feet a trench holds, the weight of bedding gravel, and the cost per foot of run. The lengths, the trench, the density and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own supplier sheet. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your site and does not know your flow, your pipe size, your cover depth, your soil or your local permit rules, and it gives no verdict that a culvert carries the water. Every number here is your own entry.

The sections truth

Sections: the run divided by the section length, rounded up, and what is left of the last one.

The trench truth

Trench: the length times width times depth, in cubic feet.

The weight truth

Weight: the cubic feet times the density, in pounds and tons.

The cost truth

Cost: the sections and gravel at their prices, and what that is per foot of run.
